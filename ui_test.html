Do you know how much your electric school bus can earn when it's parked?

This calculator is for school bus operators who want to compare the 10-year total cost of ownership of a diesel bus versus an electric bus that can also supply power back to the grid when parked — a technology called Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G). Enter your fleet details and operating schedule, and we'll show you the full cost picture, including any savings from the Fuse V2G program.
